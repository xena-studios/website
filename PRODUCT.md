# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Four kinds of visitor, usually arriving from GitHub, raptorpanel.net, or a link someone shared:

- **Server owners** who run game servers or communities and want to know who is behind Raptor before trusting it with their machines.
- **Developers** who might read the source, contribute, or self-host, and want to see how the studio works.
- **Partners and press** (hosts, game communities, writers) checking what the studio is and how to reach it.
- **Future hires and collaborators** looking at what the studio builds and how it thinks.

They are technical or close to it, skeptical of hype, and spend under a minute deciding whether the studio is real and worth following.

## Product Purpose

xenastudios.co is the studio's front door. It says what Xena Studios is in one breath, puts Raptor (the studio's only product today) in front of the visitor with honest detail and direct links, states how the studio works, and gives a way to get in touch. It grows into a home for writing later (a blog), so the structure must leave room for that without a redesign.

Success: a visitor can tell within seconds what the studio builds, can reach Raptor's site, docs, and source in one click, and leaves trusting that the claims are checkable.

## Brand Personality

**Technical, honest, open.** Writes like an engineer talking to another engineer: plain claims, real specifics, links to the source instead of adjectives. Quietly confident, never loud. The precision and polish of Linear (exact type, deliberate hairlines, motion that is felt rather than seen) applied to a studio that builds infrastructure in the open.

## Anti-references

- **Gamer RGB / neon**: esports glows, angled panels, aggressive gradients.
- **Generic SaaS**: gradient blobs, identical feature-card grids, hero metrics, "Get started free" on every block.
- **Crypto / space hype**: galaxy wallpapers, rockets, particle fields, "to the moon" futurism. The brand's horizon is one calm planet limb, nothing more.
- **Template agency site**: "We craft digital experiences", team headshot grids, buzzword copy.

## Design Principles

1. **Show the source, not adjectives.** Every claim either links to something checkable (code, docs, the product) or is specific enough to be checked. No superlatives.
2. **The brand repo is the design.** Colours, logos, and the horizon come from xena-studios/brand. The site composes them; it doesn't invent new ones.
3. **One idea per screen.** Each section makes one point and gets out of the way. Space is part of the message.
4. **Raptor stays Raptor.** When the product appears, it appears in its own identity (red raptor beside its wordmark); the studio frames it, never re-skins it.
5. **Room to grow, nothing empty.** Structure anticipates writing and more projects, but nothing ships as a placeholder.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: body text at least 4.5:1, large text at least 3:1, visible focus on every interactive element, full keyboard navigation, semantic landmarks and headings. Every animation has a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative, and no content depends on an animation finishing to become visible. Colour never carries meaning on its own.
