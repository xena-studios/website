# Product

## Users

Five kinds of visitor, usually arriving from GitHub, raptorpanel.net, Modrinth, one of our servers' sites, or a link someone shared:

- **Server owners** who run game servers or communities and want to know who is behind Raptor or our plugins before trusting them with their machines.
- **Developers** who might read the source, contribute, or self-host, and want to see how the studio works.
- **Partners and press** (hosts, game communities, writers) checking what the studio is and how to reach it.
- **Future hires and collaborators** looking at what the studio builds and how it thinks.
- **Players** who heard of MostWanted or Airfields and want to know who makes them, and where to play.

They are technical or close to it, skeptical of hype, and spend under a minute deciding whether the studio is real and worth following.

## Product Purpose

xenastudios.co is the studio's front door. It says what Xena Studios is in one breath, then splits into two paths: **tools** for the people who run servers (Raptor, NeoSkript in public beta, and the Minecraft plugins with their docs at docs.xenastudios.co) and **servers** for the people who play (MostWanted and Airfields, both in development and marked that way, each with its own site). About says how the studio works and what's open source; Contact gives every way to reach us. The home page's "Lately" list is the start of a news section, so a blog can come later without a redesign.

Success: a visitor can tell within seconds what the studio builds and which side is for them, can reach Raptor's site, docs, and source, or a server's site, in one or two clicks, and leaves trusting that the claims are checkable.

## Brand Personality

**Technical, honest, open about what's open.** Writes like an engineer talking to another engineer: plain claims, real specifics, links to the source instead of adjectives. Quietly confident, never loud. The precision and polish of Linear (exact type, deliberate hairlines, motion that is felt rather than seen) applied to a studio that builds infrastructure in the open.

## Anti-references

- **Gamer RGB / neon**: esports glows, angled panels, aggressive gradients.
- **Generic SaaS**: gradient blobs, identical feature-card grids, hero metrics, "Get started free" on every block.
- **Crypto / space hype**: galaxy wallpapers, rockets, particle fields, "to the moon" futurism. The brand's horizon is one calm planet limb, nothing more.
- **Template agency site**: "We craft digital experiences", team headshot grids, buzzword copy.

## Design Principles

1. **Show the source, not adjectives.** Every claim either links to something checkable (code, docs, the product) or is specific enough to be checked. No superlatives.
2. **The brand repo is the design.** Colours, logos, and the horizon come from xena-studios/brand. The site composes them; it doesn't invent new ones.
3. **One idea per screen.** Each section makes one point and gets out of the way. Space is part of the message.
4. **Products keep their identity.** Raptor appears as Raptor (red raptor beside its wordmark), NeoSkript as NeoSkript (its `.sk` tile beside its wordmark), MostWanted as MostWanted (its block wordmark and desert key art), Airfields as Airfields (its gold wordmark and the current season's key art), and each plugin with its own icon; the studio frames them, never re-skins them. The servers are private and proprietary, so the site never links their source or calls them open source.
5. **Room to grow, nothing empty.** Structure anticipates writing and more projects, but nothing ships as a placeholder. A project that isn't released yet says so plainly and shows something real (NeoSkript shows a script it runs), never a "coming soon" banner.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: body text at least 4.5:1, large text at least 3:1, visible focus on every interactive element, full keyboard navigation, semantic landmarks and headings. Every animation has a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative, and no content depends on an animation finishing to become visible. Colour never carries meaning on its own.
